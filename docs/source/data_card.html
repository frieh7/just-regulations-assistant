Data Card: JUST Regulations Assistant

Team Project, Phase 1 · Hamza Moeen Rasheed Essa, Mohammad Ziad Abdelmajeed Al Rousan, A'sem Zakariya Ali Elfrieh · Data lead: Mohammad Ziad Abdelmajeed Al Rousan
SourceOfficial documents of Jordan University of Science and Technology, all from the university's regulations page: https://www.just.edu.jo/aboutjust/Pages/Regulations.aspx (346 documents listed; we use 36).
  • Students (24): Degree Regulation No. 1/1987 (amended), exam regulations and exam pledge, medical excuses, disability accommodations, student discipline 2024, conduct code, admission fees and additional fees, aid fund, loans, level exams, change of major, student number, medical insurance, housing, electronic ID, transfer between universities, CIT field training, parallel program, weather suspension, exchange programs, campus entry, personal data changes.
  • Staff (12): faculty instructions, teaching load, office hours, administrative leave, attendance, unpaid leave, conferences, summer teaching, student evaluation of faculty, teaching assignments, fees for employees' children, lost and found.
The direct link to every PDF is in data/SOURCES.md (and data/sources.json).
PermissionThe documents are public: the university publishes them on its website for students and staff. We use them only for a non-commercial course project, keep them unchanged in data/raw/, and cite the document and article in every answer. If the project goes beyond the course (e.g. on the real university website), we will ask the university for written permission.
Size36 PDF files, 107 pages, about 247,000 characters of extracted text (roughly 100,000 tokens). Largest: faculty instructions (21 pages) and the degree regulation (17 pages); most others are 1 to 5 pages.
Language and formatArabic (one document, the exam pledge, is in English). PDF only. 19 files have a usable text layer; 17 are scans or use broken fonts and are read with Tesseract OCR (Arabic + English). Our script src/extract_text.py also repairs a font bug in the text-layer files that reverses the letter pair "لا" (895 corrupted words before the fix, 3 after), removes invisible direction marks and converts Arabic-Indic digits (١٤) to 14. Output: one UTF-8 text file per PDF in data/text/.
Sensitive contentNone. The documents contain general rules only: no student names, grades, records or personal contact details. We do not collect personal data, and the assistant refuses questions about a person's own record or a pending decision.
Known quality problems
  • OCR makes mistakes in numbers and article numbers (e.g. "(0)" for "(5)") and sometimes drops list items. Numbers in the test set come from text-layer pages or were checked against the PDF page image.
  • In some text-layer tables, numbers come out with reversed digits (e.g. 2017 as "7102"), mainly in the fee and unpaid-leave documents; amounts from these tables are not used until checked by hand.
  • Rules can conflict: the medical-excuse deadline is 2 working days to the Health Center (Medical Excuses, Art. 4) but 2 weeks to the dean (Degree Regulation, Art. 7(e)); the assistant should give both.
  • Some documents are old (e.g. 1987 regulation with later amendments); each answer shows its source so users can check.
Sample5 real files in data/samples/: Degree Regulation (17 pages), Exam Regulations (scanned, 5 pages), Medical Excuses Instructions (1 page), Weather Suspension Policy (1 page), Faculty Office Hours Rules (1 page). All 36 PDFs are in data/raw/ and their text in data/text/.

Seed test set: 30 questions with expected answers written from these documents before any model was run (eval/seed_test_set.csv). Repository: github.com/frieh7/just-regulations-assistant
