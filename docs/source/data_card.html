Data Card: JUST Regulations Assistant

Team Project, Phase 1 · Users: JUST bachelor students, all majors · Hamza Moeen Rasheed Essa, Mohammad Ziad Abdelmajeed Al Rousan, A'sem Zakariya Ali Elfrieh · Data lead: Mohammad Ziad Abdelmajeed Al Rousan
SourceOfficial documents of Jordan University of Science and Technology, from the university's regulations page: https://www.just.edu.jo/aboutjust/Pages/Regulations.aspx (346 documents listed; we use 36). The direct link to every PDF is in data/SOURCES.md and data/sources.json.
  • Student rules (24): Degree Regulation No. 1/1987 (amended), exam regulations and exam pledge, medical excuses, disability accommodations, student discipline 2024, conduct code, admission and additional fees, aid fund, loans, level exams, change of major, student number, medical insurance, housing, electronic ID, transfer between universities, CIT field training, parallel program, weather suspension, exchange programs, campus entry, personal data changes.
  • Staff rules (12): faculty instructions, teaching load, office hours, administrative and unpaid leave, attendance, conferences, summer teaching, student evaluation of faculty, teaching assignments, fees for employees' children, lost and found.
  • Planned next collection (not downloaded yet): study plans and course catalogs for all bachelor majors, published on www.just.edu.jo (e.g. the viewplan.aspx pages).
PermissionThe documents are public: the university publishes them on its website for its students and staff. We use them only for a non-commercial course project, keep them unchanged in data/raw/, and cite the document and article in every answer. We have not asked for written permission; we will ask the university before any use beyond the course.
Size36 PDF files, 107 pages, 43,365 words (247,425 characters) of extracted text. About 80,000 tokens with a modern tokenizer (o200k_base) or 167,000 with an older one (cl100k_base). Largest: faculty instructions (21 pages) and the degree regulation (17 pages).
Language and formatArabic PDFs (one short form, the exam pledge, is in English). 19 files have a usable text layer; 17 are scans or use broken fonts and are read with Tesseract OCR. src/extract_text.py repairs a font bug that reverses the letter pair «لا» (895 corrupted words before, 3 after), removes invisible direction marks and converts Arabic-Indic digits. Output: one UTF-8 text file per PDF in data/text/. The system works in Arabic: students ask and are answered in Arabic.
Sensitive contentNone. The documents contain general rules only: no student names, grades, records or personal contact details. We collect no personal data, and the assistant refuses questions about a person's own record or a pending decision.
Known quality problems
  • OCR misreads some numbers and article numbers (e.g. "(0)" for "(5)") and sometimes drops list items. Numbers used in the test set come from text-layer pages or were checked against the PDF page image.
  • Some text-layer tables reverse digits (e.g. 2017 as "7102"), and one file (medical insurance) maps the letter «ف» to «ا» («الاصل» [corrupted] for «الفصل» [the semester]). These files need a manual fix before their numbers are used.
  • Rules can conflict: the medical-excuse deadline is 2 working days to the Health Center (Medical Excuses, Art. 4) but 2 weeks to the dean (Degree Regulation, Art. 7(e)).
  • Some rules depend on the major or study-plan year; chunks will carry this metadata.
Sample5 real files in data/samples/: Degree Regulation (17 pages), Exam Regulations (scanned, 5 pages), Medical Excuses Instructions (1 page), Weather Suspension Policy (1 page), Faculty Office Hours Rules (1 page). All 36 PDFs are in data/raw/ and their text in data/text/.

Seed test set: 30 Arabic questions with expected answers written from these documents before any model was run (eval/seed_test_set.csv). Repository: github.com/frieh7/just-regulations-assistant
